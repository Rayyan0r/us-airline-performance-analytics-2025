# ✈️ U.S. Airline Performance Analytics 2025

An end-to-end **Data Analytics project** focused on analyzing U.S. airline operational performance, flight delays, cancellations, airports, routes, and network-level patterns using **Power BI, SQL, Python, Excel, and Power Query**.

---

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes approximately **7 million flight records** to understand airline operational performance and identify major factors contributing to delays and cancellations.

The analysis transforms raw airline data into an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to provide insights into:

- Flight volume and operational performance
- On-time performance
- Flight delays and delay causes
- Cancellation patterns
- Airport-level performance
- Route-level performance
- Network and departure-time patterns
- Operational improvement opportunities

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The main objectives of this project were to:

1. Analyze overall U.S. airline operational performance.
2. Identify major contributors to flight delays.
3. Analyze cancellation patterns and causes.
4. Identify high-delay airports and routes.
5. Analyze monthly changes in delay performance.
6. Understand airline and network-level performance.
7. Convert analytical findings into actionable operational recommendations.

## 🔑 Key Insights

### ✈️ Overall Performance

- **7M** total flights analyzed.
- **77.75%** of flights operated on time.
- **21.92%** experienced delays.
- **1.47%** of flights were cancelled.
- **0.28%** were diverted.

### ⏱️ Delay Analysis

- **Late Aircraft delays** contributed **39.19%** of total recorded delay minutes.
- **Carrier delays** contributed **32.45%**.
- **July** recorded the highest monthly delay rate at **28.74%**.
- **June** followed with a delay rate of **28.15%**.

### 🌦️ Cancellation Analysis

- **102,875 flights** were cancelled.
- Weather accounted for **61.63%** of recorded cancellations.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

### 1. Improve Turnaround Management

Focus on reducing late-aircraft propagation through improved turnaround planning and operational monitoring.

### 2. Strengthen Weather Preparedness

Use weather-risk monitoring and contingency planning during periods with higher disruption risk.

### 3. Monitor High-Delay Periods

Prioritize operational resources during departure periods with elevated delay rates.

### 4. Target High-Delay Airports & Routes

Use airport and route-level analysis to identify locations requiring additional operational attention.
